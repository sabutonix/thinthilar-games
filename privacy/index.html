Privacy policy

Thin Thi Lar Games (game.thinthilar.com) · Last updated 9 November 2026

What we keep

If you play without signing in: your game progress (puzzle results, streaks, Little Egg pets, settings) is stored only in your own browser. To show "how did everyone do today" stats and friend-group leaderboards, we store a random player ID (not linked to your name), your puzzle results, and the nickname you choose when you join a group.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook: we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that service, and we store your game progress with your account so you can continue on any phone. We never see or store your Google or Facebook password, and we don't post anything on your behalf.

How we use it

Only to run the games: saving and loading your progress, showing your name on leaderboards in groups you join, and daily stats. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads based on it, and we don't share it with anyone except the service that stores it for us (Supabase, which hosts our database).

Deleting your data

Guest progress: clear this site's data in your browser. Account data: email khantkywesoe@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my data". We delete your account and all saved progress within 30 days. You can also remove our access in your Google or Facebook account settings.

Children

The games are for everyone. Children under 13 should play with a parent's permission and should not sign in without it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions: khantkywesoe@gmail.com.
